## Internet/search-engine reference policy

Web/search-engine results were sampled visually to help establish the appearance of playing cards, court figures, Jokers and physical materials. Search engines may have included Google, Bing, Yahoo or Yandex depending on the research session.

Those services are not the underlying copyright owners. For any third-party source image record:
1. creator/rights holder;
2. original page/asset URL;
3. licence/permission;
4. access date;
5. affected project asset;
6. whether pixels were copied/traced/composited or merely viewed;
7. modifications.
